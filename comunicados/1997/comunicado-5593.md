# COMUNICADO N. 005593

```
                        COMUNICADO N. 005593
                        --------------------

                                  Divulga  notificacao do Banco Cen-
                                  tral da Grecia.

               Levamos ao conhecimento dos interessados teor de noti-
ficacao do Banco Central da Grecia, de 6 de marco de 1997:

                     "ANNOUNCEMENT

  50. - DRACHMA NOTE DATED 8.12.1978 TO BE CALLED IN

    The Bank of Greece announces that the 50.- drachma note dated 8th
December 1978 is to be called in.

    The said note is to remain legal tender up to and including  31st
May  1997.  After the time-limit has expired, namely as from 1st June
1997,  the  note  called in will cease to be legal tender but it will
still  be  accepted  by the Bank of Greece for a period of ten  years
thereafter,  no  formalities whatever being required, in repayment of
any obligation or in exchange for notes in current circulation.

     After the above-mentioned ten-year period has elapsed, namely as
from  1st  June 2007, this note will cease to have any value and will
be considered to have been annulled. No claim whatever may thereafter
be raised in respect of such notes.

     Our  announcement  relating to the issue of the note in question
is dated 25th June 1980.

                                   Athens, 17th February 1997"

                       Brasilia,  8  de abril de 1997.

                       DEPARTAMENTO DE CAMBIO

                       Jose Maria Ferreira de Carvalho
                       Chefe
```
